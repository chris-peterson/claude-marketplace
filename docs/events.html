bridge.ai · interop

Events

A plugin announces a fact it caused as one line, and whoever cares matches that line. Neither side names the other, so either one can change, ship, or be missing entirely without breaking the other. Writing those lines down is what makes the integration auditable: every key here has a named publisher, a declared body, and the list of who is listening.

The key says who caused the fact and what happened; the body is one compact JSON object, always present, {} when the event carries nothing. A subscriber matches the whole key, anchored, in a PostToolUse hook.
